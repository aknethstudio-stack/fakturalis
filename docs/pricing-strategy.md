# 💰 Pricing Strategy & Business Model

## Competitive Freemium Pricing

Fakturalis uses a competitive freemium model designed to capture market share while maintaining
healthy unit economics.

### Plan Structure

| Plan           | Price   | Target Segment              | Key Features                                 |
| -------------- | ------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| **Free**       | 0 PLN   | Solo entrepreneurs, testing | 7 invoices/month, KSeF, basic features       |
| **Smart**      | 49 PLN  | Growing SMEs                | Unlimited invoices, 3 users, JPK, automation |
| **Business**   | 99 PLN  | Established businesses      | 10 users, warehouses, OCR, API access        |
| **Enterprise** | 299 PLN | Large organizations         | Unlimited users, white-label, SLA 99.9%      |

## Unit Economics

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

- **Smart Plan**: €135 × 24 months = €675 average CLV
- **Business Plan**: €275 × 36 months = €1,100 average CLV
- **Enterprise Plan**: €690 × 48 months = €2,500 average CLV

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

- **Digital Marketing**: €60-90 per customer (Smart/Business)
- **Direct Sales**: €150-300 per customer (Business/Enterprise)
- **Referral Program**: €25-50 per customer (all plans)

### Key Metrics

- **CAC to CLV Ratio**: 1:7 to 1:12 (healthy SaaS metrics)
- **Payback Period**: 4-8 months depending on acquisition channel
- **Gross Margin**: 85%+ (typical SaaS margins)
- **Net Revenue Retention**: Target 110%+ through upgrades

## Pricing Psychology

### Anchoring Strategy

- **Free Plan**: Creates low-risk entry point
- **Smart Plan**: Positioned as "best value" (most popular)
- **Business Plan**: 2x Smart price for clear upgrade path
- **Enterprise Plan**: 3x Business price for premium positioning

### Value Communication

**Free to Smart Upgrade Triggers:**

- Invoice limit reached (7 invoices)
- Need for multiple users
- Professional branding requirements
- JPK reports necessity

**Smart to Business Upgrade Triggers:**

- Team growth (>3 users needed)
- Inventory management needs
- OCR document processing
- API integration requirements

**Business to Enterprise Upgrade Triggers:**

- Large team scaling (>10 users)
- White-label requirements
- SLA guarantees needed
- Custom integrations

## Implementation Strategy

### Launch Approach

1. **Soft Launch**: Free + Smart plans only (market validation)
2. **Beta Program**: 100 early adopters on Smart plan
3. **Full Launch**: All 4 plans with competitive pricing
4. **Market Penetration**: Aggressive Smart plan promotion

### Pricing Testing

- **A/B Tests**: Smart plan at 49 PLN vs 59 PLN
- **Feature Bundling**: Different combinations for Business plan
- **Annual Discounts**: 17% discount for yearly payments
- **Regional Pricing**: Consider CEE market adjustments

### Success Metrics

- **MRR Growth**: 20%+ month-over-month
- **Plan Distribution**: 60% Smart, 30% Business, 10% Enterprise
- **Upgrade Rate**: 25%+ free-to-paid conversion
- **Customer Satisfaction**: NPS >50 across all plans

## Risk Mitigation

### Competitive Response

- **Price Wars**: Focus on value differentiation vs price cuts
- **Feature Parity**: Maintain technology advantage through continuous development
- **Market Saturation**: Geographic expansion to other CEE markets

### Churn Prevention

- **Onboarding**: Comprehensive user education program
- **Support Quality**: Tiered support based on plan level
- **Feature Adoption**: Track usage and proactively suggest upgrades
- **Feedback Loop**: Regular customer surveys and feature requests

This pricing strategy positions Fakturalis as a premium value alternative in the Polish invoicing
market, leveraging modern technology and superior user experience to justify competitive pricing
while maintaining strong unit economics.
